<h2 class="category">Industrial Projects</h2>
</div>

We work with industry partners on applied research, knowledge transfer, and demonstration projects that bridge the gap between academic research and real-world implementation. Mechanisms that can support this work include Innovate UK Knowledge Transfer Partnerships, Ofgem SIF and NIA projects, IDLA DPhil studentships, prosperity partnerships, and industrial fellowships offered by the Royal Academy of Engineering and the Royal Society. If you have a challenge that could benefit from our expertise, we would be pleased to discuss how we might work together.

<div class="projects">
<h2 class="category">Technology Licensing</h2>
</div>

<!-- OUI - Data Centre GPU Tech --->

We are committed to the wide adoption of our research, and the majority of our methods, software tools and models are published openly and made freely available. Where research has led to patentable innovations which require commercial development to achieve impact, licensing supports knowledge transfer and provides a sustainable pathway towards deployment at scale. 

GridFlexDC (patent-pending) is a system we developed enabling AI data centres to provide high-value grid frequency regulation services, and other forms of fast time-scale flexibility, through intelligent GPU power capping. Reinforcement learning is used to minimise the impact on quality-of-service. If you are interested in licensing GridFlexDC, please get in touch.
